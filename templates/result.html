⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This system is an AI-assisted academic document analysis prototype. It combines OCR extraction, visual comparison, image-quality analysis, machine-learning pattern analysis, tampering indicators, and academic consistency checks.

An anomaly score, similarity score, tampering score, or overall verification score does not independently prove that a document is fraudulent or authentic.

Final authenticity decisions should be made using authoritative institutional records and appropriate manual verification.
